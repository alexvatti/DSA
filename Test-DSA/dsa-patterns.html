Guidelines (For Test Your Skills)

1. Spin the wheel and take the number it lands on.
2. The timer starts when the wheel stops. Set 30 minutes at most.
3. Think first, then analyse, then complete the solution.
4. Produce all the answers inside the time.
5. Turn on similar problems to also solve 2 related problems for each one.
6. Do not copy and paste the code.
7. Do not run to the solutions.
